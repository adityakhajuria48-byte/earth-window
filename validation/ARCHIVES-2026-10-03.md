## Limits

ASTER in this particular archive covers 2000–2006. HLS begins in 2020 here. MSS holdings cover 1972–2013. Amazonia-1 SR starts in 2024 and CBERS-4A MUX SR in 2026. INPE holdings are regional, not guaranteed worldwide. CBERS HRC is Level-2, not orthorectified Level-4. Pixel spacing is not equivalent to optical/radar resolving power. No new unrestricted ISRO or national Chinese/Japanese account-gated portal access is claimed.
